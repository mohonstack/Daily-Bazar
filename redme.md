# 🛒 বাজার দর | BazarDor

**BazarDor (বাজার দর)** is a modern price-tracking and product comparison web application.  
It allows users to explore daily market prices, compare products across categories and stores, view price changes, and manage their account through a simple and user-friendly interface.

---

## 🚀 Technologies Used

- **Next.js**
- **React.js**
- **TypeScript**
- **Tailwind CSS**
- **HeroUI**
- **Better Auth**
- **MongoDB**
- **React Hot Toast**
- **Vercel**

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. 📊 Daily Product Price Tracking
Users can view current product prices along with previous prices, price trends, and percentage changes.

### 2. 🛍️ Category-Based Product Browsing
Products are organized into different categories such as rice, dal, oil, vegetables, fish, meat, dairy products, and spices.

### 3. 🔍 Product Details & Store Comparison
Users can open individual product pages to view detailed information and compare prices from available stores.

### 4. 🔐 Authentication & User Profile
Users can create accounts, sign in using email/password, Google, or GitHub, and update their profile information.

### 5. ⚡ Dynamic & User-Friendly Experience
The application includes dynamic routes, loading skeletons, custom 404 pages, toast notifications, protected routes, responsive design, and smooth navigation.

---

## 🎯 Project Purpose

The goal of **BazarDor** is to make everyday market prices easier to understand and compare.  
Users can quickly check product prices and price changes from one platform instead of searching multiple sources.
